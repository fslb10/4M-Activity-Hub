Your Unit 1.12 toolkit

Morality: the principles, values and virtues guiding right and wrong.

Ethics: careful reasoning about what we ought to do, especially in difficult cases.

Conscience: a person's reasoned judgment applying moral truth to a particular choice. It needs formation.

Where does Catholic moral truth come from?

God is the ultimate source of goodness. Scripture, Tradition, Church teaching and natural law help us discover and apply that truth, not simply follow our feelings. See Unit 1.12, CCC 1777 and 1785.

What if AI gives a confident answer?

Speed and confidence are not evidence of a formed conscience. Ask what the answer assumes, who is affected and who will take responsibility.
